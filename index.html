What is a virtual number?

A virtual number is a phone number that can be managed through an online service rather than being tied directly to a traditional physical SIM card.

How do I get a number?

Create an account, browse the available numbers, select a supported service and review the price before completing your purchase.

Will every service accept a virtual number?

No. Acceptance depends on the individual service and its policies. Wavynum should only be used with services that permit virtual numbers.

Can I use Wavynum for account verification?

Only where the relevant service permits the use of virtual numbers. Wavynum is not intended to bypass identity checks, KYC requirements or security controls.

What happens if a number is unavailable?

Availability can change quickly. If a number is no longer available, you can browse the other numbers currently listed in your account.
